### The Roadmap to International Engineering

The path to a foreign engineering career requires more than just technical skills; it requires a mindset of continuous evolution. My roadmap involves three key pillars:

1.  **Technical Mastery:** Achieving a deep understanding of full-stack development and database integrity. I aim to become an expert in creating scalable back-end solutions that are as stable as the physical laws we study in our UET Physics labs.
2.  **Cultural Intelligence:** An international engineer must be a global citizen. I am actively working on my communication skills and learning about the professional ethics and standards expected in foreign tech industries.
3.  **Research and Innovation:** I want to contribute to open-source projects and research that has international relevance. Whether it is optimizing AI algorithms or improving data security, my work must meet global benchmarks.

Exposure to a foreign country offers a unique perspective on "Process." In my time at UET, I have learned the value of grit and making the most of available resources. I want to see how different cultures approach a bug or a hardware failure, and I want to contribute my own unique problem-solving style to those global teams.
